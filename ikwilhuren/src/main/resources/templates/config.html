What you can afford

MVGM won't allocate you a home unless your income covers a multiple of the yearly rent. Not a preference — a request above the line can't be won, and it wastes one of your five.

Your ceiling is €1400 a month. Anything dearer is skipped.

Applying

MVGM lets you hold five viewing requests at once. Every home is scored on whether it can still be won, how fresh it is, and how well it suits you — and your five are always kept on the five best. When a better home appears, the weakest is traded in for it.

MVGM stops accepting requests once a home is full — every home we applied to carrying its "many viewing requests" warning came straight back as "no more viewings possible". Those still count as long shots (MVGM re-draws if their first choice walks), so they're held, but they lose their slot to any home you could properly win. A request MVGM has already drawn is never touched.
